# Dogs of War — Elite Guns and Hired Guns (v0.1)

Decisions 50–51. Every Elite Gun carries two of seven archetypes. Each archetype has its own upgrade list, and an Elite Gun may take **one upgrade** from either of its two lists. Seven archetypes make 21 pairs, and each pair appears exactly once, so no two Elite Guns share the same two archetypes.

Prices come from `03-points-formula.md` (1 Requisition = 1 N5 point, with CC counted at 0.25 a point). The profiles are original. N5 informed the ranges and the standouts, such as the S5 heavy weapons platform, the S6 melee beast, the S4 hunter, thermo-optic heavy infantry and impersonators, but none copies an N5 unit.

## Archetypes and their upgrades

- **Marksman**, precision shooting at range. Steady Aim (BS +1) 2; Marksmanship (Marksmanship) 4; Triangulated Fire (Triangulated Fire) 3; X-Visor (X-Visor) 2; Multispectral Visor (Multispectral Visor) 3.
- **Gunner**, volume of fire: support weapons, suppression, AROs. Total Reaction (Total Reaction) 5; Smoke Grenades (Smoke Grenades) 3; Heavy Pistol (Heavy Pistol) 2; Steady Hands (BS +1) 2; Grenades (Grenades) 2.
- **Brawler**, close combat. Close-Quarters Drills (CC +3) 2; Berserk (Berserk) 2; Natural Born Warrior (Natural Born Warrior) 2; DA CC Weapon (DA CC Weapon) 2; Guard (Guard) 2.
- **Infiltrator**, stealth, camouflage and surprise. Surprise Attack (Surprise Attack (-3)) 2; Stealth (Stealth) 2; Decoy (Decoy) 3; Infiltration (Infiltration) 3; Silenced Pistol (Silenced Pistol) 2.
- **Specialist**, medics, engineers, hackers and observers: mission tools. Specialist Training (WIP +1) 2; Forward Observer (Forward Observer) 2; Repeater (Repeater) 2; Deployable Repeater (Deployable Repeater) 2; Specialist Operative (Specialist Operative) 2.
- **Juggernaut**, toughness: armour, wounds, staying power. Reinforced Armour (ARM +1) 3; No Wound Incapacitation (No Wound Incapacitation) 5; Nanoscreen (Nanoscreen) 4; Immunity (Shock) (Immunity (Shock)) 2; Courage (Courage) 2.
- **Skirmisher**, speed and movement: jumping, climbing, flanking. Super-Jump (Super-Jump) 2; Climbing Plus (Climbing Plus) 2; Sixth Sense (Sixth Sense) 2; Bangbomb (Bangbomb (+3)) 2; Combat Jump (Combat Jump) 2.

Upgrade prices have a minimum of 2. An Elite Gun isn't offered an upgrade that only repeats a skill it already has.

### Concepts

- **Overwatch:** Watches a lane and punishes anything that crosses it.
- **Gunslinger:** Twin-pistol duelist who is as dangerous at arm's length as at 16 inches.
- **Stalker:** Camouflaged sniper who infiltrates forward and takes the first shot.
- **Designator:** Long-range spotter who marks targets for the company's heavy guns.
- **Siege Rifleman:** Armoured sniper who doesn't need to hide.
- **Rooftopper:** Gets above the fight and shoots down into it.
- **Breacher:** First through the door: shotgun, chain-colt and a blade.
- **Ambusher:** Hidden support gun that springs a crossfire.
- **Sapper:** Combat engineer who fixes TAGs, mines lanes and blows doors.
- **Colossus:** S5 armoured heavy-weapons platform: the company's walking bunker.
- **Outrider:** Bike-mounted gunner who swings round a flank.
- **Shiv:** Disguised assassin: walks up looking friendly and cuts the target down.
- **Sawbones:** Battlefield surgeon who has learned to hold the line around the wounded.
- **Ravager:** S6 melee monster: huge, fast and nearly impossible to stop once it closes.
- **Pouncer:** S4 hunter that leaps across the table and onto its prey.
- **Ghostwire:** Infiltrating hacker who hunts enemy hackers from inside their lines.
- **Phantom:** Heavy armour under thermo-optic camouflage: tough and invisible.
- **Pathfinder:** Drops in from an unexpected edge and opens a route.
- **Marshal:** Armoured officer who can take the Lieutenant role and keeps the company fighting.
- **Dispatch Rider:** Bike-mounted medic and courier who reaches objectives first.
- **Vaulter:** Jump-pack heavy trooper who lands where the fight is thickest.

Every Elite Gun can **Start in Reserve**. The **Marshal** can also take Lieutenant or NCO. The **Ravager** and **Pouncer** are Impetuous.
