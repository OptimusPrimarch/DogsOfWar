# Dogs of War — Hired Guns Baseline (v0.1)

Decision 47: the Hired Gun stat line is the **floor** for every attribute. Pilots, Elite Guns and chassis are built up from it, never below it.

**Source.** Every light-infantry unit that N5 classes as Line Troops or Garrison Troops, across all armies in the official Infinity Army data (2026-10-14): 31 units, 23 Line and 8 Garrison. Each unit is counted once. The figures below are medians and counts; no unit is named.

- **Orders:** 27 of 31 are Regular in N5. Hired Guns are Irregular (#12), which is cheaper by the formula (about 1.5 against 4).
- **Skills:** none is standard. The most common, Courage and Terrain, appear on 7 of 31.
- **Kit:** a CC Weapon (27 of 31) and a Pistol (24 of 31), with a Combi Rifle as the usual gun (15 of 31).

## The Hired Gun stat line

| MOV | CC | BS | PH | WIP | ARM | BTS | VITA | S | Orders |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4-4 | 14 | 11 | 10 | 13 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 2 | Irregular |

Every template also carries a **Pistol** and a **CC Weapon**, as nearly every N5 line trooper does. The template adds its own weapon or specialist skill on top (#38: specialist templates carry nothing more).

Valued with `03-points-formula.md`, a Hired Gun with a Combi Rifle is worth about **10 Requisition**, but it costs 0 (#35). That's why the in-play cap (#41) matters.

## Using it as the floor

- **Pilots and Elite Guns** start from this line and add value through attributes, skills and kit, priced with the cheat sheet.
- **Never below the floor.** No profile has an attribute lower than the Hired Gun's, unless a deliberate trade-off is priced in. For example, lower PH for a heavier weapon.
