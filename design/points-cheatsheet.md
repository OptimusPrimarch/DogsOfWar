# Points Cheat Sheet

**1 Requisition = 1 N5 point** (decision 46). The standard game is **200 Requisition**. Hired Guns are free.

These costs are inferred from N5's own prices, which typically land within about 2 points of the real thing. The full model and tables are in `03-points-formula.md`.
