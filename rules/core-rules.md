# Dogs of War — Core Rules

**Draft.** Rules are added here once they're decided. The decisions and the reasoning behind them live in [`design/00-foundations.md`](../design/00-foundations.md). Anything not yet written out is marked *to be written*.

## How the rules fit together

Dogs of War is played on the **Infinity N5** rules. Anything this document doesn't mention works exactly as N5 says. This document covers only what Dogs of War changes or adds, and where the two disagree, this document wins.

- **The N5 rules** are on the official [Infinity wiki](https://infinitythewiki.com) (© Corvus Belli). The chapters are linked below.
- **Weapons, skills and equipment:** every one in the catalogue is listed in [armory.md](armory.md), with a short summary and a link to its official rule.
- **Our own rules** are written in our own words. Rules brought back from the previous edition, N4, are marked **(N4)**.

## 1. The setting

Players command **mercenary companies** in the Infinity universe. The game should feel like Infinity to anyone who has played it.

No faction or unit names are used anywhere. Dogs of War shares Infinity's skills, equipment and weapons, and adds its own when a specific need calls for it.

## 2. Building a company

| | |
|---|---|
| **Allocation** | 60 or 100 Requisition |
| **Pilot and TAG** | Exactly one pilot, fully custom, and one custom TAG. The TAG is built from a chassis (S6, S7 or S8), components, and one Signature slot. |
| **Elites** | Up to 5, including any held in reserve. Each is a fixed profile plus one kit item. Command Tokens can raise the limit. A second pilot is an expensive Elite option. |
| **Troopers** | Free (0 Requisition) and Irregular. They share one profile, and templates change only their equipment. |
| **Lieutenant** | Required, and costs Requisition. The pilot and some Elites can take it. NCO costs more than Lieutenant. |
| **On the table** | At most 1 TAG or pilot, plus 10 infantry. A reserve TAG called in by a second pilot is the only way to field a second TAG. |

The catalogue enforces these limits. Prices are *to be written*.

## 3. Round structure

A game lasts **3 rounds**. Each round runs through these phases in order. Each phase matches a unit category in the list, with TAG and Pilot acting together. In each phase, the player with initiative takes a full N5 Active Turn while the opponent reacts with AROs, and then the roles swap. Only the models that belong to the current phase may spend Orders.

1. **Impetuous:** Impetuous troops.
2. **Pilot:** pilots and the TAG they're in, including a second pilot.
3. **Elite:** Elites.
4. **Trooper:** Troopers.
5. **Reinforcement:** new arrivals deploy, then act with their own Orders. Arriving isn't an action.

The player with initiative goes first in every phase. The Lieutenant Order can be spent in any phase.

## 4. Orders

- Your pilot and each Elite give a Regular Order to a shared pool. Any model eligible in the current phase can spend from it.
- Troopers are Irregular. Each Trooper's own Order is spent only on itself, in the Trooper phase.
- A pilot's Tactical Awareness Order is spent in the Pilot phase.
- Pool Orders left unspent carry on into later phases, and Troopers can use them in the Trooper phase.

**Tracking the pool with tokens:**
1. At the start of the round, take one Order token for each of your Regular models.
2. Discard a token each time you spend a Regular Order.
3. At the start of each of your turns within a phase, discard one unspent token for each Regular model you've lost since your previous turn.

## 5. Command

- **Command Tokens:** the N5 starting pool, slightly enlarged (number *to be written*). They are never regained. On top of everything N5 allows, they pay for drop pods, replacement TAGs, extra Elite slots and calldowns.
- **Suppressive Fire** works exactly as in N5.

## 6. Reinforcements and calldowns

Reinforcements arrive in the Reinforcement phase. Each one uses the first of these methods that's possible:

1. **Drop pod (costs 1 Command Token):** lands anywhere on the table. It scatters and damages whatever it lands on (*to be written*).
2. **Deployment (Tactical):** see below.
3. **Your deployment zone**, if neither of the above is possible.

> **Deployment (Tactical)** — *Dogs of War skill.* Every reinforcing model has it. Place the model within the Zone of Control of an allied model and outside the LoF of every enemy.

- Elite reserves are chosen before the game. Trooper reserves are unlimited. The only limit on arrivals is the 10-infantry cap.
- **Replacement TAGs:** during the Pilot phase, a pilot can call in a TAG from the stock lineup, which is the same for every company. It costs Command Tokens, roughly most of the pool. Prices *to be written*.
- **Calldowns:** orbital and aerial support, built on N5's [Speedballs](https://infinitythewiki.com/Request_Speedball). The list is *to be written*.

## 7. Pilots and TAGs

- **Mount and dismount (N4):** N5 dropped the rules for getting in and out of a TAG, so Dogs of War brings back N4's version with one change: mounting or dismounting is free with any movement skill. *Full text to be written.*
- **Eject:** uses N5's [Transmutation](https://infinitythewiki.com/Transmutation) (Escape System). The profile the TAG turns into is the company's pilot.
- **Jockey:** *Dogs of War rule.* Pilots only. A pilot that ends any movement in base contact with an enemy TAG makes a face-to-face WIP roll against it. *Results to be written.*
- **Unmanned TAGs:** a pilot can climb into an enemy TAG whose pilot has dismounted, and take it over.
- **Building the TAG:** a chassis (S6, S7 or S8), weapons and systems on its mounts, and one Signature slot. Capacity keeps each build coherent. There is no Heat. The catalogue lays out the options.

## 8. Not used from N5

- **Fireteams.** N5's Fireteam rules aren't used, so skills that only work inside a Fireteam (FT Master, Number 2, Strategic Deployment, and the Fireteam parts of others) have no effect. The word "fireteam" may still appear loosely in descriptions.
- **Exrah** and **Shasvastii** (skills named after factions).

## 9. Table and miniatures

- The table is **36" × 36"**, with very dense terrain.
- Corvus Belli miniatures are recommended, using N5 silhouettes for every model.

## Adding to these rules

- **A new rule:** write it here in our own words, and record the decision and the reasoning in `design/00-foundations.md`.
- **A new weapon, profile or piece of gear:** add it in the catalogue with the NewRecruit Editor. Builds don't touch hand-made entries, unless one has the same name as a wiki weapon. They appear in [armory.md](armory.md) marked †.
- **Beyond Infinity:** new skills, equipment and weapons are fine when they meet a specific need. Give them names that don't clash with N5 terms, and never use faction or unit names.
