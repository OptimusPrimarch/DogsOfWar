## Dogs of War rules

- **[Round Structure](core-rules.md#3-round-structure)**: Three rounds. Each round runs Impetuous, Pilot (pilots and their TAG), Elite, Trooper, then Reinforcement. In each phase the player with initiative takes a full N5 Active Turn, then the other player does; only that phase's models spend Orders. The Lieutenant Order can be spent in any phase.
- **[Order Pool](core-rules.md#4-orders)**: Pilots and Elites give Regular Orders to one pool, spent by whoever acts in the current phase. Troopers are Irregular and act on their own Orders in the Trooper phase. Track the pool with tokens: one per Regular model at the start of the round, discard one per Order spent, and at the start of each of your turns discard one per Regular model lost since your last turn.
- **[Command Tokens](core-rules.md#5-command)**: The N5 starting pool, slightly enlarged (number to be set), never regained. Besides N5's uses they pay for drop pods, replacement TAGs, extra Elite slots and calldowns.
- **[Reinforcements](core-rules.md#6-reinforcements-and-calldowns)**: Arrivals come in the Reinforcement phase, by the first method possible: a drop pod (1 Command Token) anywhere on the table, Deployment (Tactical), or your deployment zone. Elite reserves are chosen before the game; Trooper reserves are unlimited. The 10-infantry cap is the only limit.
- **[Deployment (Tactical)](core-rules.md#6-reinforcements-and-calldowns)**: Every reinforcing model has this skill. Place the model within the Zone of Control of an allied model and outside the LoF of every enemy.
- **[Replacement TAGs](core-rules.md#6-reinforcements-and-calldowns)**: During the Pilot phase a pilot can call in a TAG from the stock lineup, the same for every company, paid with Command Tokens (roughly most of the pool). Lineup and prices to be written.
- **[Mount and Dismount (N4)](core-rules.md#mount-and-dismount-n4)**: N5 dropped this; Dogs of War brings back N4's version. Getting in: a pilot that ends a skill with the Movement label in base contact with the TAG climbs in at the end of that Order. Getting out: at the start of an Order, right after declaring its first skill with the Movement label, the pilot starts that movement anywhere in base contact with the TAG, using its own profile for the whole Order. Neither costs anything extra. Not while Engaged or Immobilized, and never through Cautious Movement from a TAG. Getting in or out cancels Hidden Deployment and marker states. Game states carry across, except Unconscious and Possessed; a Possessed TAG's pilot can't get in or out. A pilot can still leave an Unconscious TAG. While its pilot is out, the TAG acts as the pilot's Peripheral; while the pilot is in a Null state, the TAG is Pilotless instead. While aboard, the pilot can't be hacked or hit by E/M. N4 let pilots board only their own TAG; Dogs of War adds Jockey and Unmanned TAGs.
- **[Jockey](core-rules.md#7-pilots-and-tags)**: Pilots only. A pilot that ends any movement in base contact with an enemy TAG makes a face-to-face WIP roll against it. Results to be written.
- **[Unmanned TAGs](core-rules.md#7-pilots-and-tags)**: A pilot can climb into an enemy TAG whose pilot has dismounted, and take it over.
- **[Pilotless State](core-rules.md#pilotless-state)**: A TAG becomes Pilotless when its pilot enters a Null state (Unconscious, Dead…). It isn't a Null state itself: the TAG stays in play, counts for scoring and can be targeted. It can't generate or spend Orders, but it can still declare AROs, and its player can spend a Command Token to give it Total Reaction (how long that lasts is to be written). Cancelled when a pilot climbs in, or when its own pilot leaves the Null state.
- **[Eject](core-rules.md#7-pilots-and-tags)**: A TAG ejects its pilot through N5's Transmutation (Escape System). The profile the TAG turns into is the company's pilot.

## Common Skills

- **[Alert](https://infinitythewiki.com/Alert)** *(Automatic Skill · Optional, No Roll)*: If the trooper or an ally in its ZoC is attacked and it hasn't acted that Order, it may turn to face any direction at the end of the Order. No roll, no AROs.
- **[BS Attack](https://infinitythewiki.com/BS_Attack)** *(Short Skill / ARO · Attack)*: Shoot with a ranged weapon, skill or equipment using BS (or whichever attribute the weapon names). LoF is needed unless the weapon says otherwise, and Burst can be split between targets.
- **[Cautious Movement](https://infinitythewiki.com/Cautious_Movement)** *(Long Skill · Movement, No Roll)*: Move up to your first MOV value without drawing AROs, provided you start and finish outside every enemy's LoF and ZoC, and outside enemy hacking areas if you're Hackable.
- **[CC Attack](https://infinitythewiki.com/CC_Attack)** *(Short Skill / ARO · Attack)*: Fight an enemy in base contact with a CC-trait weapon, rolling CC. Each success forces a saving roll.
- **[Climb](https://infinitythewiki.com/Climb)** *(Long Skill · Movement, No LoF, No Roll)*: Move up or across a vertical surface, up to your first MOV value plus 2 inches. No roll.
- **[Discover](https://infinitythewiki.com/Discover)** *(Basic Short Skill / ARO)*: Reveal an enemy marker by passing a WIP roll modified as if you were shooting it. A trooper that fails can't try the same marker again that turn.
- **[Dodge](https://infinitythewiki.com/Dodge)** *(Short Skill / ARO · Movement)*: Roll PH, face to face against the attacker, to avoid every attack in the Order (not comms attacks). Success lets you move up to 2 inches.
- **[Idle](https://infinitythewiki.com/Idle)** *(Basic Short Skill · No Roll)*: Do nothing. It still counts as activating, so it can draw AROs, and it's what happens when a declared skill turns out to be illegal.
- **[Intuitive Attack](https://infinitythewiki.com/Intuitive_Attack)** *(Long Skill · BS Attack)*: With an Intuitive Attack weapon, pass an unmodified WIP roll to take a single shot at a target you can't see because of zero visibility or a marker state.
- **[Jump](https://infinitythewiki.com/Jump)** *(Long Skill · Movement, No LoF, No Roll)*: Leap in any direction, including an arc, up to your first MOV value plus 2 inches, landing somewhere at least as big as your base. No roll.
- **[Look Out](https://infinitythewiki.com/Look_Out)** *(ARO · No Roll)*: An ARO for a trooper that sees an enemy act: every other trooper on your side may turn to face, by passing a Dodge roll at PH-3.
- **[Move](https://infinitythewiki.com/Move)** *(Basic Short Skill · Movement, No LoF, No Roll)*: Move up to your first MOV value. Declaring Move again in the same Order adds the second value.
- **[Place Deployable](https://infinitythewiki.com/Place_Deployable)** *(Short Skill / ARO · Attack)*: Put a deployable weapon, piece of equipment or ancillary peripheral on the table in base contact, or anywhere along your path in the Active Turn. No roll.
- **[Reload](https://infinitythewiki.com/Reload)** *(Short Skill / ARO · Attack)*: While in the ZoC of an ally carrying Baggage, clear your Unloaded state or restore every spent use of your Disposable kit. One trooper per Order.
- **[Request Speedball](https://infinitythewiki.com/Request_Speedball)** *(Automatic Skill · Optional)*: Spend two Speedball tokens to drop two supply pods onto the table using the Combat Jump procedure (PH 15). Each pod carries a Speedball Chart item: one chosen, one rolled.
- **[Reset](https://infinitythewiki.com/Reset)** *(Short Skill / ARO · No LoF)*: Roll WIP, face to face against the attacker's WIP, to shrug off every comms attack and Guided attack in the Order.
- **[Speculative Attack](https://infinitythewiki.com/Speculative_Attack)** *(Long Skill · BS Attack)*: With a Speculative Attack weapon, lob a single shot at a target out of LoF along a possible trajectory. A circular template can land off-centre as long as it still covers the target.
- **[Suppressive Fire](https://infinitythewiki.com/Suppressive_Fire)** *(Long Skill · Attack)*: Pick a weapon with the Suppressive Fire trait and enter the Suppressive Fire state with it.

## Special Skills

- **[Aerial](https://infinitythewiki.com/Aerial)** *(Automatic Skill · Obligatory)*: The trooper is airborne. It can't go Prone or be Engaged, never counts as touching models, objectives or scenery, can't use Cautious Movement, and doesn't set off Boost weapons.
- **[Berserk](https://infinitythewiki.com/Berserk)** *(Long Skill · Attack, CC Attack, CC Special Skill, Movement, Optional)*: One Long Skill: charge up to both MOV values combined into base contact with a target in LoF, then attack in close combat, adding any bonus in brackets. If the charge can't reach, the trooper Idles.
- **[Bioimmunity](https://infinitythewiki.com/Bioimmunity)** *(Optional)*: When making a saving roll against ARM or BTS, the trooper may use whichever of the two values is better. The attack's other effects still apply. Not against Comms Attacks.
- **[Booty](https://infinitythewiki.com/Booty)** *(Deployment Skill · Optional)*: When the trooper is placed on the table, roll on the Booty Chart for a bonus item on top of its normal gear.
- **[Camouflage](https://infinitythewiki.com/Camouflage)** *(Automatic Skill · Optional)*: The trooper can deploy as a Camouflage marker, and can go back into the Camouflaged state during the game.
- **[Chain of Command](https://infinitythewiki.com/Chain_of_Command)** *(Automatic Skill · Optional, Private Information)*: If your Lieutenant is Isolated or in a Null state when Loss of Lieutenant is checked, this trooper takes over as Lieutenant on the spot and the check counts as passed.
- **[Climbing Plus](https://infinitythewiki.com/Climbing_Plus)** *(Automatic Skill · Movement, Optional)*: Climb becomes a Short Skill when it's the first one declared in an Order, and Move or Dodge can carry the trooper up and across walls as if they were floors.
- **[Combat Instinct](https://infinitythewiki.com/Combat_Instinct)** *(Automatic Skill · Optional)*: Enemy Surprise Attack penalties don't apply to this trooper.
- **[Combat Jump](https://infinitythewiki.com/Combat_Jump)** *(Long Skill · Airborne Deployment (AD), Private Information, Optional)*: Airborne Deployment: the trooper waits off the table, then on its own Order is placed on any open horizontal surface and rolls PH. On a pass it lands there; on a fail it arrives at its own table edge as a model.
- **[Commlink](https://infinitythewiki.com/Infinity_Reinforcements#Commlink)** *(Obligatory)*: Lets the army keep a Reinforcements Section and call it in with Request Reinforcements, even after the Commlink trooper falls. A bracketed bonus raises the army's trooper limit. One per army.
- **[Counterintelligence](https://infinitythewiki.com/Counterintelligence)** *(Automatic Skill · Optional, Private Information)*: Blunts the enemy's strategic use of Command Tokens: they cancel only one of your Orders, or your Command Token allowance rises to two.
- **[Courage](https://infinitythewiki.com/Courage)** *(Automatic Skill · Optional)*: The trooper may pass any Guts roll automatically and ignores Retreat!, though Loss of Lieutenant still affects it.
- **[Cyberplug](https://infinitythewiki.com/Cyberplug)** *(Automatic Skill · Obligatory)*: The trooper can take Cyberplug Peripherals.
- **[Decoy](https://infinitythewiki.com/Decoy)** *(Deployment Skill · Optional)*: The trooper can deploy in the Decoy state.
- **[Doctor](https://infinitythewiki.com/Doctor)** *(Short Skill · Optional)*: In base contact with an Unconscious trooper that has VITA, pass a WIP roll to heal one wound and bring it round. A failed roll kills the patient.
- **[Dogged](https://infinitythewiki.com/Dogged)** *(Automatic Skill · Optional)*: When it falls Unconscious, the trooper can keep fighting as if unhurt for the rest of that Player Turn, then dies in the States Phase. Any further wound kills it at once, and it can't be healed.
- **[Engineer](https://infinitythewiki.com/Engineer)** *(Short Skill · Optional)*: In base contact with a target that has STR, pass a WIP roll to repair one wound, repeating as often as needed. A failed roll adds a wound instead.
- **[Explode](https://infinitythewiki.com/Explode)** *(Automatic Skill · Attack, Obligatory)*: When knocked Unconscious, the trooper detonates at the end of the Order: a circular template centred on it, Shock ammunition at PS 7. Then it's removed as Dead.
- **[Exrah](https://infinitythewiki.com/Exrah)** *(Automatic Skill · Obligatory)*: The trooper can't survive being knocked down: the moment it falls Unconscious it dies outright, with no extra wound, so nothing can heal it. Works even while it's in a Null state.
- **[Forward Deployment](https://infinitythewiki.com/Forward_Deployment)** *(Deployment Skill · Superior Deployment, Optional)*: The trooper can deploy up to the bracketed distance beyond your Deployment Zone.
- **[Forward Observer](https://infinitythewiki.com/Forward_Observer)** *(Short Skill / ARO · BS Attack, Optional)*: A ranged attack rolled with WIP that leaves its target Targeted instead of forcing a saving roll.
- **[Frenzy](https://infinitythewiki.com/Frenzy)** *(Automatic Skill · Obligatory, States Phase)*: Once the trooper wounds or kills an enemy, it gains Impetuous and Limited Cover for the rest of the game, and drops out of any marker state.
- **[FT Master](https://infinitythewiki.com/FT_Master)** *(Automatic Skill · Obligatory)*: While the trooper is in a Fireteam, every member counts as Regular. If they already were, the Team Leader's coherency range grows by 2 inches.
- **[G: Jumper](https://infinitythewiki.com/G:_Jumper)** *(Automatic Skill · Obligatory)*: The trooper fields two or three Proxy bodies that share one Order and count as one trooper. Only the active Proxy acts, and control can jump to another Proxy when one goes down.
- **[Guard](https://infinitythewiki.com/Guard)** *(Automatic Skill · Optional)*: The trooper can make CC Attacks against an enemy in its LoF and ZoC without reaching base contact, and both sides use their contact-based close combat skills as normal.
- **[Hacker](https://infinitythewiki.com/Hacker)** *(Automatic Skill · Obligatory)*: The trooper can carry a Hacking Device and run the programs it grants, run some programs without one, and take Upgrade Programs. Works even while the trooper is in a Null state.
- **[Hidden Deployment](https://infinitythewiki.com/Hidden_Deployment)** *(Deployment Skill · Optional, Private Information)*: The trooper can deploy in the Hidden Deployment state, with its position kept secret.
- **[Immunity](https://infinitythewiki.com/Immunity)** *(Automatic Skill · Obligatory)*: The trooper ignores the special effects of whatever is in brackets, such as an ammunition type or a trait, treating that ammunition as Normal. Wider versions cover every ARM-based or BTS-based attack.
- **[Impersonation](https://infinitythewiki.com/Impersonation)** *(Deployment Skill · Optional, NFB, Superior Deployment)*: Deploy disguised as an Impersonation marker anywhere outside the enemy Deployment Zone, or inside it by passing a WIP roll. A failed roll puts the trooper at its own table edge as a model.
- **[Impetuous](https://infinitythewiki.com/Impetuous)** *(Automatic Skill · Impetuous Phase, Obligatory)*: The trooper gets a free activation in the Impetuous Phase, limited to a set menu of actions and forced to rush the nearest enemy or the enemy Deployment Zone.
- **[Infiltration](https://infinitythewiki.com/Infiltration)** *(Deployment Skill · Superior Deployment, Optional)*: Deploy anywhere in your own half, or in the enemy half (outside their Deployment Zone) by passing a PH-3 roll. A failed roll puts the trooper at its own table edge as a model.
- **[Infinity Spec-Ops](https://infinitythewiki.com/Infinity_Spec-Ops)** *(Automatic Skill · Optional)*: A customisable trooper: at list building, add two options from the Spec-Ops Chart. In play it can switch to its Enhanced Profile by collecting a SpecBall. One per army.
- **[Inspiring Leadership](https://infinitythewiki.com/Inspiring_Leadership)** *(Automatic Skill · Obligatory)*: Improves Fireteam coherency. While its owner is the Lieutenant and on the table, every Order-giving trooper is Regular with Courage, Coordinated Orders cost only the Lieutenant Order, and the army ignores Retreat!.
- **[Journalist](https://infinitythewiki.com/Journalist)** *(Automatic Skill · Optional)*: Allies in the trooper's ZoC get +3 to Guts rolls. Enemies in its ZoC get -3.
- **[Lieutenant](https://infinitythewiki.com/Lieutenant)** *(Automatic Skill · Private Information, Obligatory)*: The army's commander, with a Special Lieutenant Order. If it is Isolated, in a Null state or off the table at the Tactical Phase, Loss of Lieutenant applies. Some profiles add extras such as +1 Command Token or +1 Order.
- **[Limited Cover](https://infinitythewiki.com/Limited_Cover)** *(Automatic Skill · Obligatory)*: Attacks against this trooper ignore the -3 penalty for Partial Cover.
- **[Marksmanship](https://infinitythewiki.com/Marksmanship)** *(Automatic Skill · Obligatory)*: When the trooper makes a BS Attack, it ignores the penalties from Partial Cover and Nanoscreen.
- **[Martial Arts](https://infinitythewiki.com/Martial_Arts)** *(Automatic Skill · CC Special Skill, Optional)*: Gives the close combat bonuses for the level listed on the profile, as set out in the Martial Arts Chart.
- **[MetaChemistry](https://infinitythewiki.com/MetaChemistry)** *(Deployment Skill · Optional)*: When the trooper is placed on the table, roll once on the MetaChemistry Table for a bonus MOD or extra skill.
- **[Mimetism](https://infinitythewiki.com/Mimetism)** *(Automatic Skill · NFB, Obligatory)*: Enemies shooting at the trooper with LoF, or trying to Discover it, take the bracketed penalty, such as -3 or -6. It doesn't apply in close combat.
- **[Minelayer](https://infinitythewiki.com/Minelayer)** *(Deployment Skill · Optional)*: When the trooper deploys, it can also place a deployable weapon or piece of equipment (or the bracketed number of them) in its ZoC, provided no enemy or camouflage marker is in the trigger area.
- **[Mnemonica](https://infinitythewiki.com/Mnemonica)** *(Automatic Skill · Optional)*: When the trooper enters a Null state, its WIP, this skill and any Lieutenant role pass to an ally on the table that has a Cube or is a Remote. The original is then removed as Dead.
- **[Morpho-Scan](https://infinitythewiki.com/Morpho-Scan)** *(Short Skill · Comms Attack, No Roll, Optional, Zone of Control)*: No roll needed: the trooper copies the MOV, CC, BS and PH of an enemy with VITA in its ZoC. It counts as a Comms Attack, and a target that tries to Reset takes -9.
- **[Natural Born Warrior](https://infinitythewiki.com/Natural_Born_Warrior)** *(Automatic Skill · CC Special Skill)*: When the trooper is attacked in close combat and fights back, it ignores all the negative MODs its opponent would impose.
- **[NCO](https://infinitythewiki.com/NCO)** *(Automatic Skill · Optional)*: The trooper can turn Special Lieutenant Orders into Tactical Orders and use them, from any Combat Group. As a Fireteam leader, it can activate the team with them.
- **[Neurocinetics](https://infinitythewiki.com/Neurocinetics)** *(Automatic Skill · Obligatory)*: In its Active Turn, all the trooper's ranged weapons fire at Burst 1. In the Reactive Turn, it can fire its full Burst at a single target.
- **[No Cover](https://infinitythewiki.com/No_Cover)** *(Automatic Skill · Obligatory)*: The trooper never benefits from Partial Cover.
- **[No Wound Incapacitation](https://infinitythewiki.com/No_Wound_Incapacitation)** *(Automatic Skill · Optional)*: When it falls Unconscious, the trooper carries on as if unhurt. One more wound kills it, though it can still be healed back to normal.
- **[Non-Hackable](https://infinitythewiki.com/Non-Hackable)** *(Automatic Skill · Obligatory)*: Hacking programs that target specific troop types (HI, REM, TAG and so on) can't target this trooper, even while it is in a Null state.
- **[Number 2](https://infinitythewiki.com/Number_2)** *(Automatic Skill · Optional)*: If the Fireteam Leader is Isolated or in a Null state, this trooper can take over as leader immediately.
- **[Parachutist](https://infinitythewiki.com/Parachutist)** *(Long Skill · Airborne Deployment (AD), Optional, Private Information)*: Airborne Deployment: the trooper waits off the table, then on its own Order enters at any table edge outside the enemy Deployment Zone, with no Partial Cover that Order. The (Deployment Zone) version can also enter inside it.
- **[Paramedic](https://infinitythewiki.com/Paramedic)** *(Automatic Skill · Obligatory)*: The trooper carries a MediKit for reviving Unconscious allies.
- **[Peripheral](https://infinitythewiki.com/Peripheral)** *(Automatic Skill · Obligatory)*: Covers a Controller and its Peripherals (secondary models): they count as one trooper, deploy together and act on the same Order, though each can pick its own target.
- **[Protheion](https://infinitythewiki.com/Protheion)** *(Automatic Skill · CC Special Skill, Optional)*: In close combat, each wound the trooper inflicts on a target with VITA heals one of its own. If it's unhurt, its VITA rises instead, up to a limit.
- **[Regeneration](https://infinitythewiki.com/Regeneration)** *(Automatic Skill · States Phase, Optional)*: In the States Phase, roll PH. A pass heals one wound and can end Unconscious; a fail adds one.
- **[Religious Troop](https://infinitythewiki.com/Religious_Troop)** *(Automatic Skill · Obligatory)*: The trooper passes Guts rolls automatically and holds its ground, though it may roll WIP to fall back as if it had failed. It ignores Retreat!, but Loss of Lieutenant still applies.
- **[Remdriver](https://infinitythewiki.com/Remdriver)** *(Deployment Skill · Optional)*: On deployment, tag one Remote already on the table. That Remote gains the bonuses listed on this trooper's profile until this trooper enters a Null state.
- **[Remote Presence](https://infinitythewiki.com/Remote_Presence)** *(Automatic Skill · Obligatory)*: A trooper with STR gets two levels of Unconscious, so it dies one wound later than usual. A single Engineer or GizmoKit success clears all the wounds keeping it down.
- **[Request Reinforcements](https://infinitythewiki.com/Infinity_Reinforcements)** *(Obligatory)*: With a Commlink trooper, keep a Reinforcement Section off the table and bring it in after taking casualties or in the final round, through an extra Request Reinforcements step in the turn.
- **[Sapper](https://infinitythewiki.com/Sapper)** *(Deployment Skill / Long Skill · Optional)*: The trooper can deploy in the Foxhole state, or dig into it later as a Long Skill, as long as there's room for its template.
- **[Sensor](https://infinitythewiki.com/Sensor)** *(Short Skill · Attack, Optional, Zone of Control)*: A WIP+6 roll that reveals every hidden or camouflaged enemy in the trooper's ZoC at once, without needing LoF. Enemies can't re-camouflage there, and Discover rolls against camouflage markers get +6.
- **[Shasvastii](https://infinitythewiki.com/Shasvastii)** *(Automatic Skill · Obligatory)*: When the trooper falls Unconscious it becomes an embryo instead. It still counts for Retreat! and scenario conditions during the game, but not at final scoring, and Shock ammunition kills it outright.
- **[Sixth Sense](https://infinitythewiki.com/Sixth_Sense)** *(Automatic Skill · Optional)*: The trooper can react to attacks from outside its LoF as if it saw all around, ignores the -6 for shooting through zero visibility, and takes no penalties to Dodge or Reset except those from Immobilized and Isolated.
- **[Specialist Operative](https://infinitythewiki.com/Specialist_Operative)** *(Automatic Skill · Optional)*: The trooper counts as a Specialist for mission and scenario rules.
- **[Stealth](https://infinitythewiki.com/Stealth)** *(Automatic Skill · Optional)*: In its Active Turn, the trooper can use Cautious Movement inside enemy ZoC and hacking areas, and its movement only draws AROs from enemies who can see it.
- **[Strategic Deployment](https://infinitythewiki.com/Strategic_Deployment)** *(Deployment Skill · Optional)*: As a Fireteam's leader, the trooper deploys first and brings the whole team forward as if it had Forward Deployment (+4 inches).
- **[Strategos](https://infinitythewiki.com/Strategos)** *(Automatic Skill · Optional)*: As Lieutenant, hold back two troopers to deploy after your opponent instead of one, and convert Special Lieutenant Orders into Regular Orders.
- **[Super-Jump](https://infinitythewiki.com/Super-Jump)** *(Automatic Skill · Movement, Optional)*: Jump becomes a Short Skill when declared first in an Order, so the trooper can jump and shoot in one Order. A Jump taken as a Long Skill covers the first MOV value plus 4 inches. No Partial Cover while jumping.
- **[Surprise Attack](https://infinitythewiki.com/Surprise_Attack)** *(Automatic Skill · Obligatory)*: Attacking from marker form or Hidden Deployment in its Active Turn, the trooper imposes the bracketed penalty on its targets. It must return to marker form before it can do so again.
- **[Tactical Awareness](https://infinitythewiki.com/Tactical_Awareness)** *(Automatic Skill · Optional)*: The trooper generates an extra Tactical Order, for its own use only, on top of its usual Order.
- **[TAGCom](https://infinitythewiki.com/TAGCom)** *(Automatic Skill · Optional)*: While on the table and not in a Null state, the trooper gives every TAG in its Combat Group the bonuses listed on its profile.
- **[Tech-Recovery](https://infinitythewiki.com/Tech-Recovery)** *(Automatic Skill · Optional)*: When hit by an allied GizmoKit, pass a PH roll to clear every state an Engineer could fix, such as Immobilized or Isolated. It doesn't cover Unconscious.
- **[Technorganic](https://infinitythewiki.com/Technorganic)** *(Automatic Skill · Optional)*: Doctor, Engineer, MediKit or GizmoKit can each revive this trooper from Unconscious, whatever its wound attribute.
- **[Terrain](https://infinitythewiki.com/Terrain)** *(Automatic Skill · Optional)*: In terrain of the listed type, the trooper gets +1 inch on its first MOV value and ignores that terrain's movement restrictions.
- **[Total Reaction](https://infinitythewiki.com/Total_Reaction)** *(Automatic Skill · Optional)*: The trooper can shoot AROs with its weapon's full Burst.
- **[Transmutation](https://infinitythewiki.com/Transmutation)** *(Automatic Skill)*: The trooper has a second profile and switches to it when the bracketed condition is met, such as taking a set number of wounds. Wounds and states carry over. Escape System is the version for bailing out of a vehicle.

- **[Disposable (X)](https://infinitythewiki.com/Traits#Disposable_.28X.29)**: The item has X uses, and declaring it spends one whether or not it works.

- **[Indiscriminate](https://infinitythewiki.com/Traits#Indiscriminate)**: The item can be used or deployed even with camouflage markers in its area, or with no valid target nearby.

- **[Non-Lethal](https://infinitythewiki.com/Traits#Non-Lethal)**: The weapon never causes wounds or saving rolls, whatever ammunition it uses. Only its other effects apply.
- **[Non-Reloadable](https://infinitythewiki.com/Traits#Non-Reloadable)**: Spent uses of this Disposable item can never be recovered.
- **[Perimeter](https://infinitythewiki.com/Traits#Perimeter)**: The item is placed anywhere in the user's ZoC instead of in base contact.
- **[Prior Deployment](https://infinitythewiki.com/Traits#Prior_Deployment)**: The item must be placed on the table during the Deployment Phase.
- **[Reflective](https://infinitythewiki.com/Traits#Reflective)**: The effect also works on troopers with Marksmanship, a Multispectral Visor, or similar.
- **[Silent (X)](https://infinitythewiki.com/Traits#Silent_.28X.29)**: Attacking from inside the target's ZoC but outside its LoF gives the target the bracketed penalty to Dodge.
- **[Speculative Attack (Trait)](https://infinitythewiki.com/Traits#Speculative_Attack)**: The weapon can be used for Speculative Attacks.

## Labels

- **[Airborne Deployment (AD)](https://infinitythewiki.com/Labels#Airborne_Deployment_.28AD.29)**: Groups the skills for airborne troops, so a rule that mentions Airborne Deployment covers all of them.
- **[Assignable (Transmutation)](https://infinitythewiki.com/Labels#Assignable_.28Transmutation.29)**: At deployment only, the item can be handed to a trooper with Transmutation that's on the table as a model. It stays in base contact with that trooper and moves with it.
- **[Attack](https://infinitythewiki.com/Labels#Attack)**: Using it counts as an Attack, and Attacks can't target allies or neutral elements.
- **[BS Attack (Label)](https://infinitythewiki.com/Labels#BS_Attack)**: Using it counts as a BS Attack.
- **[CC Attack (Label)](https://infinitythewiki.com/Labels#CC_Attack)**: Using it counts as a CC Attack.
- **[CC Special Skill](https://infinitythewiki.com/Labels#CC_Special_Skill)**: Works only in base contact with the target. Its MODs stack with other CC Special Skills, in both the Active and Reactive Turn.
- **[Comms Attack](https://infinitythewiki.com/Labels#Comms_Attack)**: An attack you avoid with Reset, not Dodge. Partial Cover doesn't help against it.
- **[Comms Equipment](https://infinitythewiki.com/Labels#Comms_Equipment)**: Equipment that certain Hacking Programs and ammunition can affect.
- **[Hackable](https://infinitythewiki.com/Labels#Hackable)**: Allies and enemies can target it with certain Hacking Programs.
- **[Hostile](https://infinitythewiki.com/Labels#Hostile)**: A game element outside every army list, with its own attributes. Every player treats it as an enemy, and it can attack and be attacked.
- **[Marker](https://infinitythewiki.com/Labels#Marker)**: Can be shown on the table as a marker instead of a model or token.
- **[Movement](https://infinitythewiki.com/Labels#Movement)**: Using it counts as Movement.
- **[Negative Feedback (NFB)](https://infinitythewiki.com/Labels#Negative_Feedback_.28NFB.29)**: Only one NFB skill, item or program can be active at a time. Switching one on switches off any other.
- **[No LoF](https://infinitythewiki.com/Labels#No_LoF)**: Doesn't need LoF to the target.
- **[No Roll](https://infinitythewiki.com/Labels#No_Roll)**: Works automatically, without a roll. Any fixed result is shown in brackets.
- **[Non-Reloadable (Label)](https://infinitythewiki.com/Labels#Non-Reloadable)**: Spent uses can never be recovered, and Unloaded can't be cleared.
- **[Null](https://infinitythewiki.com/Labels#Null)**: A state that stops the trooper giving an Order or counting for scoring: Dead, Disconnected, Possessed, Sepsitorized and Unconscious.
- **[Obligatory](https://infinitythewiki.com/Labels#Obligatory)**: Its use is mandatory.
- **[Optional](https://infinitythewiki.com/Labels#Optional)**: Its use is optional.
- **[Private Information](https://infinitythewiki.com/Labels#Private_Information)**: You don't have to reveal it until it's used or the game ends.
- **[States Phase](https://infinitythewiki.com/Labels#States_Phase)**: Used only in the States Phase, unless stated otherwise.
- **[Superior Deployment](https://infinitythewiki.com/Labels#Superior_Deployment)**: Groups the skills that let troopers deploy outside their Deployment Zone, so a rule that mentions Superior Deployment covers all of them.
- **[Supportware](https://infinitythewiki.com/Labels#Supportware)**: Hacking Programs that support allied troopers or hinder enemies rather than attacking directly.

## Ammunition

- **[Armor Piercing (AP) Ammunition](https://infinitythewiki.com/Armor_Piercing_(AP)_Ammunition)**: Halves the target's ARM or BTS (rounding up) for its saving rolls. Each failure is a wound.
- **[Combined Ammunition](https://infinitythewiki.com/Combined_Ammunition)**: Ammunition written with a plus sign (such as AP+DA) applies all its parts' effects at once.
- **[Combined Saving Roll](https://infinitythewiki.com/Combined_Saving_Roll)**: Some weapons force saving rolls against more than one attribute (ARM and BTS, for example). A critical hit adds one more roll against ARM.
- **[Double Action (DA) Ammunition](https://infinitythewiki.com/Double_Action_(DA)_Ammunition)**: Two saving rolls per hit, both mandatory. Each failure is a wound.
- **[Eclipse Ammunition](https://infinitythewiki.com/Eclipse_Ammunition)**: Smoke that also blinds Multispectral Visors: a zero-visibility zone no visor can see through.
- **[Electromagnetic (E/M) Ammunition](https://infinitythewiki.com/Electromagnetic_(E/M)_Ammunition)**: Two saving rolls per hit against half BTS. A failure leaves the target Isolated, unable to take Orders from the pool.
- **[Explosive (EXP) Ammunition](https://infinitythewiki.com/Explosive_(EXP)_Ammunition)**: Three saving rolls per hit, all mandatory. Each failure is a wound.
- **[Normal (N) Ammunition](https://infinitythewiki.com/Normal_(N)_Ammunition)**: One saving roll per hit. Each failure is a wound, and a critical hit adds a saving roll.
- **[Paralysis (PARA) Ammunition](https://infinitythewiki.com/Paralysis_(PARA)_Ammunition)**: One PH-6 roll per hit. A failure leaves the target Immobilized-A. No effect on targets without PH.
- **[Shock Ammunition](https://infinitythewiki.com/Shock_Ammunition)**: One saving roll per hit. Against a target with VITA 1, a failure kills outright instead of leaving it Unconscious.
- **[Smoke Ammunition](https://infinitythewiki.com/Smoke_Ammunition)**: Lays a circular zero-visibility zone of unlimited height until the States Phase. It needs no target, and lets the user oppose attacks whose LoF passes through it.
- **[Stun Ammunition](https://infinitythewiki.com/Stun_Ammunition)**: One saving roll per hit. A failure leaves the target Stunned and fails its Guts roll automatically.
- **[T2 Ammunition](https://infinitythewiki.com/T2_Ammunition)**: One saving roll per hit, and each failure causes two wounds.

## States

- **[Camouflaged State](https://infinitythewiki.com/Camouflaged_State)** *(Marker)*: The trooper is on the table as a Camouflage marker, showing its Mimetism value if it has one. Enemies can't move into contact with it or attack it until it's revealed. Re-entering the state takes a Long Skill out of enemy LoF.
- **[Dead State](https://infinitythewiki.com/Dead_State)** *(Null)*: The trooper took more wounds than it can survive. It's removed from the table, gives no more Orders and counts for nothing at scoring.
- **[Decoy State](https://infinitythewiki.com/Decoy_State)** *(Marker)*: When the trooper deploys, it also places the bracketed number of Decoy markers (one or two) within coherency, so the opponent can't tell which is real.
- **[Disconnected State](https://infinitythewiki.com/Disconnected_State)** *(Null)*: A Peripheral cut off from its Controller (the Controller is Isolated or down, a coherency check failed, or an attack caused it). It can't act or ARO, its automatic skills stop working, and it scores nothing.
- **[Engaged State](https://infinitythewiki.com/Engaged_State)**: Models in base contact with an enemy are locked in close combat. They can only Berserk, CC Attack, Dodge, Idle, Reset or use close combat skills, and can't see outside the fight. Shooting into a close combat follows special rules.
- **[Foxhole State](https://infinitythewiki.com/Foxhole_State)**: The trooper is dug in: at least Silhouette 3, Partial Cover from every direction, Mimetism (-3) and Courage, but it can't move while it stays in the foxhole.
- **[Hidden Deployment State](https://infinitythewiki.com/Hidden_Deployment_State)**: The trooper deploys with no model or marker on the table. Its position is written down secretly, and until it's revealed it doesn't count as being on the table.
- **[Holoecho State](https://infinitythewiki.com/Holoecho_State)** *(Marker)*: The trooper is shown as up to three Holoecho markers, only one of them real. It can re-enter the state in the States Phase, or with a Long Skill, while out of enemy LoF.
- **[HoloMask State](https://infinitythewiki.com/HoloMask_State)**: The trooper is shown as a different model of the same Silhouette, disguising what it really is while it plays with its own profile.
- **[Immobilized-A State](https://infinitythewiki.com/Immobilized-A_State)**: The trooper can't act or ARO except to Dodge at PH-6, though it still provides its Order. It can be cleared by the means the full rule lists.
- **[Immobilized-B State](https://infinitythewiki.com/Immobilized-B_State)**: The trooper can't act or ARO except to Reset at WIP-3, though it still provides its Order.
- **[Impersonation State](https://infinitythewiki.com/Impersonation_State)** *(Marker)*: The trooper is disguised as an Impersonation marker. Enemies can't move into contact with it, and can't attack it at all while it's at IMP-1, until it's revealed.
- **[Isolated State](https://infinitythewiki.com/Isolated_State)**: The trooper is cut off from command: it can't receive Orders from the pool, becomes Irregular if it stays Isolated, and can't join a Fireteam.
- **[Normal State](https://infinitythewiki.com/Normal_State)**: The default state: the trooper provides its Order and counts for scoring. A trooper returns to it once every Null state is cleared.
- **[Possessed State](https://infinitythewiki.com/Possessed_State)** *(Null)*: The trooper has been taken over. Its owner can't activate it, it gives no Order, and it counts as an enemy to its own side and an ally to whoever possessed it.
- **[Prone State](https://infinitythewiki.com/Prone_State)**: The trooper is lying down. It can go prone or stand at the start or end of a movement skill (not Jump), which changes its Silhouette and how far it moves. Unconscious troopers go prone automatically, except TAGs, vehicles and riders.
- **[Retreat! State](https://infinitythewiki.com/Retreat_State)**: Once the army is in Retreat!, each surviving trooper can only use Basic Short Skills, Cautious Movement, Dodge or Reset, and drops out of any marker state.
- **[Sepsitorized State](https://infinitythewiki.com/Sepsitorized_State)** *(Null)*: The trooper's mind has been taken over: it leaves its Combat Group, can't receive Orders, and counts as an enemy to its own side.
- **[Stunned State](https://infinitythewiki.com/Stunned_State)**: The trooper can't attack and takes -3 to every roll except saving rolls.
- **[Suppressive Fire State](https://infinitythewiki.com/Suppressive_Fire_State)**: The trooper covers an area with its weapon's SF Mode profile. Enemies within 24 inches take -3 in face-to-face rolls against it, and it can ARO with the full Burst 3 at a single target.
- **[Targeted State](https://infinitythewiki.com/Targeted_State)**: The trooper has been painted for strikes. Ranged attacks, Comms Attacks and Discover against it get +3, it takes -3 to Reset, and it loses Cautious Movement and Stealth.
- **[Unconscious State](https://infinitythewiki.com/Unconscious_State)** *(Null)*: The trooper has taken as many wounds as its VITA or STR. It can't act or ARO, gives no Order, its automatic skills stop working, it goes Prone, and it scores nothing until it's healed.
- **[Unloaded State](https://infinitythewiki.com/Unloaded_State)**: The trooper has used up a Disposable item and can't use it again until it Reloads, or recovers in the States Phase, next to an ally with Baggage.
